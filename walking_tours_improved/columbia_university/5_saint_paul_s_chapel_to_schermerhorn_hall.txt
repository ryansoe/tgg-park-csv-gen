Walking tour from: Saint Paul's Chapel (40.80798666411735, -73.9611608522562)
Destination: Schermerhorn Hall (40.8084381300034, -73.96054778652473)
Total distance: 270 ft
Estimated time: 1 min

Directions:

Walk to the footpath.

Start at Saint Paul's Chapel and walk northeast on the footpath for about 47 feet.
Next, turn right onto the footpath and continue for 57 feet.
Then, make a sharp left onto the footpath and walk for 170 feet. As you walk, you'll pass the historic Alma Mater on your right and the Curl on your left. Keep an eye out for the Tightrope Walker, Three-Way Piece, No. 1: Points, and Bellerophon Taming Pegasus sculptures, all on your right, as well as the lovely Gatehouse Garden on your left.

You will arrive at Schermerhorn Hall.

Enjoy your walk!

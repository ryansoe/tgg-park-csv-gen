Walking tour from: Three Sisters Statue (39.72936696078181, -121.84566743472685)
Destination: Tree of Peace (39.728802550658784, -121.84508906486849)
Total distance: 240 ft
Estimated time: 1 min

Directions:

Walk to the footpath.

Start at the Three Sisters Statue and walk southeast on the footpath for about 120 feet. You'll pass by the beautiful George Petersen Rose Garden on your right.
Next, take a slight right onto the footpath and continue for another 120 feet. Keep an eye out for the Wildcat Sculpture on your right as you walk.

You will arrive at the Tree of Peace.

Enjoy your walk!

Walking tour from: Veterans Memorial
Destination: AirSpace Museum
Total distance: 0.61 mi
Estimated time: 13 min

Directions:

Walk to the trail.

Start at the Veterans Memorial and walk along the trail for about 82 feet.
Continue straight for another 34 feet.
Turn right onto Zoo Place and walk for 30 feet.
Next, turn left onto Park Boulevard and continue for 770 feet. You'll pass the Balboa Park Carousel and the San Diego Zoo Miniature Railroad on your left.
After that, turn right onto Village Place and walk for 550 feet.
Continue on Village Place for an additional 27 feet.
Finally, turn right onto Old Globe Way and walk for 530 feet until you reach your destination.

You will arrive at the AirSpace Museum.

Enjoy your walk!
